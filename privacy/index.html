Privacy

Privacy policy

Short version: no cookies, no ad trackers, nothing from Google until you click a video, and your email is used for one thing only, my newsletter. Last updated 16 October 2026.

Who is responsible

This site is run by Marcin Struzik, who is the controller of the personal data described here. Questions or requests: hello@imstruzik.com.

The newsletter and the free library

When you sign up on the home page, on Free downloads or in the office game, you tick a box to agree to receive the newsletter (it is never ticked in advance, and the form does not send without it). Your email address then goes to GetResponse, the service that sends my newsletter on my behalf (GetResponse S.A., based in Poland, acting as my processor). You then get a confirmation email, and nothing is sent until you confirm (double opt-in). GetResponse also records when and from where you signed up and confirmed, and whether emails are opened or clicked.

I use your address to send the newsletter, about every two weeks, plus the short welcome series and the link to the free library. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email, or by writing to me. I keep your address until you unsubscribe. I never sell or share it.

The office game and its leaderboard

The game at /play/ runs entirely in your browser. If you choose to save your score, the nickname you type and your score are sent to a small service I run on Cloudflare (a Worker with a key-value store) and shown publicly in the top 10, so please do not use your real name or anything personal. No email address, IP address or other identifier is stored with it. Your nickname is also kept in your browser's local storage so you do not have to retype it. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), and you can ask me at hello@imstruzik.com to remove an entry. To keep the board clean and the service safe, nicknames are filtered and the number of saves is rate-limited, which can briefly involve technical request data on Cloudflare's side.

Analytics without cookies

I count visits with Umami. It sets no cookies, does not track you across sites and does not store your IP address. I see aggregated numbers: which pages are read, where visitors come from, the type of device, and a few clicks such as a PDF download or a newsletter signup. The script is loaded from Umami's servers (cloud.umami.is). The legal basis is my legitimate interest in knowing what is useful on this site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Because analytics stores nothing on your device and the site itself sets no cookies, there is no cookie banner; the only third-party content that can store data in your browser is a YouTube video, and it loads only when you click one (see below). If you would rather not be counted at all, a content blocker or your browser's “Do Not Track” setting stops it.

Other services the pages load

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub processes your IP address to deliver the pages and keep them secure. GitHub privacy statement.
  • Fonts. The typefaces (Archivo and JetBrains Mono) are stored on this site itself, so no connection to Google Fonts is made.
  • Reels. The short videos on the home page are hosted on this site and only load when you click one, so no third party is contacted. The link to my Instagram profile only opens instagram.com if you click it; Instagram then handles your data under its own policy.
  • Email. If you write to me, your message is stored in my mailbox and used only to reply.

The Email Blur extension

Email Blur works entirely in your browser. It does not send anything anywhere; it only remembers its own on/off setting. The source code is public on GitHub.

Affiliate links

A few links are affiliate links, for example to Screen Studio. They are always marked as such. If you buy through one, I earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. The shop, not this site, handles that purchase and its data.

Your rights

You can ask me for access to your data, its correction or deletion, a copy of it, or to restrict or object to its processing. Write to hello@imstruzik.com and I will answer within a month. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority; in Poland that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).
