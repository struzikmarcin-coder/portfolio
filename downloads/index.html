Email Blur

Blurs every email address on the page, live, before you hit record. I built it for my own product videos: one visible customer email used to mean a blur mask in the edit, tracked by hand, take after take. How to install in Chrome (1 minute)
  1. Unzip it and move the email-blur folder somewhere permanent (not Downloads, not iCloud or OneDrive).
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the email-blur folder.
  4. Pin it from the puzzle icon. One click on the envelope turns blurring on or off.

Edge, Brave, Arc and Firefox steps, plus the limits worth knowing before you record, are in the guide on GitHub, next to the full source code. It runs locally, needs one permission (to remember on/off) and sends nothing anywhere.
