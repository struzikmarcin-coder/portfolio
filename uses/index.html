Screen and video

My screen recorder for product videos. I use it constantly and recommend it without reservation. This is an affiliate link: if you buy through it I earn a small commission, at no extra cost to you. I would recommend it either way.

After Effects

Where the keyframes still live. I have animated in it for 14+ years, and AI tools now sit around it rather than replacing it.

Email Blur

A browser extension I built for myself, so no customer email ever shows up on screen while I record. Free in the library.

The AI tools that stayed

The full reasoning, including what I dropped, is in The AI Tools for Video I Stopped Using, and the 4 I Would Keep. The short version:

ChatGPT

Copy, briefs, research and strategy in one window. It stays because it covers the most different jobs, not because any single output is perfect.

ElevenLabs

Placeholder voiceover and localization into other languages without re-recording.

Seedance 2.0

Animating a generated still when the stock shot I need does not exist.

WhisperX

Captions that are right enough that a human only touches the last 10%.

Claude

Longer-form writing and the skills I built around my own workflow. More in the free tools note.
