04 — About

Marcin Struzik

I’ve spent the past 13+ years working in SaaS marketing — starting as an OG After Effects animator, cutting keyframes by hand long before AI tools existed. These days I work at the intersection of both: traditional motion design and AI, scripting, video editing and UI animation, all in one workflow.

Most of what I know came from shipping the same thing over and over: a feature, a story, a deadline — first by hand, now faster wherever AI actually earns its place. I write about that in the newsletter: what works on screen, what AI gets wrong, and why the script still matters more than the software.
